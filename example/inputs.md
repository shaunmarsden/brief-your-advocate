# Worked Example: A Fictional Project Proposal

An entirely fictional workplace scenario. You have proposed building an internal tool to automate a manual reporting process. You will be on leave when it needs presenting, so a colleague, Jordan, has agreed to present it to two people on your behalf.

## The Evidence Established

- The manual process currently takes roughly six hours a week across the team, confirmed by a two-week time log
- A working prototype exists and has been tested by two team members, both confirming it saves the estimated time
- The exact hosting cost is still being quoted by IT; not yet confirmed

## Who Jordan Is Presenting To

- **Priya Shah, VP of Engineering.** In an earlier hallway conversation, Priya specifically mentioned she is wary of any new internal tool adding to the team's maintenance burden long term.
- **Tom Reyes, Finance Director.** Nothing has actually been said by or about Tom regarding this proposal specifically. His title suggests budget is likely a concern, but that has not been confirmed by anything he has actually said.
