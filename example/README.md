# Worked Example

A fictional workplace scenario: briefing a colleague, Jordan, to present a project proposal to two people you cannot meet with directly, one with a known concern, one with only an assumed one based on their title.

- [inputs.md](inputs.md): the evidence, what's outstanding, and what is and is not actually known about each person Jordan is presenting to
- [output.md](output.md): the briefing [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) produces
- [review.md](review.md): whether the known concern got used and the assumed one got correctly flagged as unconfirmed, and whether the outstanding cost stayed outstanding
