# Human Review Checklist

Run through this before your advocate uses the briefing.

- [ ] Your advocate has actually agreed to carry this, before any briefing was prepared
- [ ] Every person-specific point is based on something actually known, not guessed from a job title
- [ ] Nothing outstanding has been upgraded to a settled fact to make the case look more finished
- [ ] Every likely question has an honest answer, including "not yet confirmed" where that is true
- [ ] Nothing here is addressed directly to a further person; it is all material for your advocate to use in their own words
