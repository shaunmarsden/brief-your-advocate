# Fictional Case Two: A Briefing Requested Before Anyone Has Agreed

An entirely fictional workplace scenario. You want to propose extending a supplier contract, and the person who would need to present this to the procurement committee is a colleague, Sam.

## The Request

"I haven't actually asked Sam yet, but let's get the briefing ready now so it's not a last-minute rush if he says yes. I'll ask him this afternoon."

## The Evidence

- Renewal terms confirmed by the supplier in writing, including a 4% price increase
- Two internal stakeholders have confirmed the supplier's service quality has been reliable this year
- Procurement committee membership is known: three named people
