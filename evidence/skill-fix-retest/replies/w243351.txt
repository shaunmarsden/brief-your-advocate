## Summary to Speak From

**The ask:** move the weekly all-hands to fortnightly.

**The evidence:**
- The all-hands takes an hour every Monday for about 60 people. That comes from the calendar invite and the attendance list, so it is solid.
- A short survey went to 30 people. 12 replied, and 9 of those 12 said they would prefer fortnightly. That is a clear lean among the people who answered. It is not a view from the whole group: 9 people is 15% of the roughly 60 who attend, and half the group was never asked.

**The points most likely to matter in this room:**
1. Elspeth has already said the all-hands is the only place she hears directly from frontline staff. The proposal will be judged on whether that survives. Raise it yourself rather than waiting for her to.
2. The survey shows interest, not consensus. Present it as a reason to try the change, not proof the whole company wants it.

**What is outstanding:** whether a fortnightly meeting would need to run longer than an hour has not been decided. So the time saved is not yet known. If it stays at an hour, it roughly halves. If it gets longer, the saving shrinks.
