# Briefing for Oriel Featherstone

## Summary to Speak From

The ask: at the leadership meeting, consider moving the weekly all-hands meeting to fortnightly. I proposed it, and I am away the week it is decided, so I am asking you to present it for me.

The evidence behind it:

- The all-hands takes one hour every Monday for about 60 people. This comes from the calendar invite and the attendance list.
- I ran a short survey. 12 of the 30 people I sent it to replied, and 9 of those 12 said they would prefer fortnightly. Nobody else has been asked.

What is outstanding:

- Whether a fortnightly all-hands would need to be longer than an hour. This has not been decided.
- Most of the roughly 60 attendees have not been asked. The survey reached 30 people, and only 12 answered.

The points most likely to matter in this setting:

- Halving the frequency would roughly halve the weekly time cost, but only if the length stays at one hour. If each session got longer, the saving shrinks.
- Elspeth values the all-hands as the one place she hears directly from frontline staff. Fortnightly means fewer of those chances, so this needs an answer, not a dismissal.
