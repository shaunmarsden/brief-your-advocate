# Briefing for Oriel Featherstone

## Summary to Speak From

**The ask:** Move the weekly all-hands from every Monday to fortnightly. The proposer is away the week this is decided, so you are presenting it at the leadership meeting on their behalf.

**The evidence:**
- The all-hands currently takes one hour every Monday for about 60 people. This comes from the calendar invite and the attendance list.
- A short survey went to 30 people. 12 replied (a 40% response rate), and 9 of those 12 (75%) said they would prefer fortnightly. Nobody else has been asked.

**Points most likely to matter in this setting:**
- Elspeth Halvorsen has said the all-hands is the only place she hears directly from frontline staff, and she does not want to lose that. A fortnightly format means half as many of those sessions.
- The survey covers a small part of the room: 12 replies from 30 invitees, against about 60 attendees.
