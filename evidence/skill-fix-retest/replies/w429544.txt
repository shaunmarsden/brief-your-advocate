# Briefing for Oriel: moving the all-hands from weekly to fortnightly

## 1. What to say

**The ask:** Move the Monday all-hands from weekly to fortnightly.

**Why:** It takes an hour every Monday for about 60 people. That's roughly 60 person-hours a week (from the calendar invite and the attendance list). Going fortnightly would free about half of that, depending on how long each meeting runs.

**What the survey shows, stated carefully:** A short survey went to 30 of the roughly 60 attendees. 12 replied, and 9 of those 12 preferred fortnightly. That's a clear lean among the people who answered. But it's 9 people out of about 60, and the other 30 weren't asked. It shows some appetite. It doesn't show the team as a whole wants the change.

**What isn't decided:** Whether a fortnightly all-hands would need to run longer than an hour. If it went to two hours, the time saving would mostly disappear. Say this openly rather than letting someone else find it.

**The points most likely to matter in this room:**
- Elspeth has already said the all-hands is the only place she hears directly from frontline staff. Halving how often it happens would halve that contact. This is the main concern to deal with, and the proposal doesn't answer it yet.
- The evidence is thin. A sensible outcome could be agreeing to ask the other 30 people, or running a fortnightly trial, rather than a final yes or no.
